Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple calculation using a person's height and weight. The formula is BMI = kg/m2 where kg is a person's weight in kilograms and m2 is their height in metres squared.

About Nitro Gym

Where Legends Are Forged

Nitro Gym was founded on the principle that true strength comes from discipline. Our state-of-the-art facility is designed to push you beyond your limits and help you achieve what you once thought was impossible.

We believe in the power of community and the importance of pushing through discomfort to reach your goals. At Nitro, you're not just joining a gym - you're joining a family of like-minded individuals committed to excellence.

Our motto, "Pain of discipline, pain of regret your choice," embodies our philosophy. The temporary discomfort of hard work is nothing compared to the lifelong regret of never trying.
